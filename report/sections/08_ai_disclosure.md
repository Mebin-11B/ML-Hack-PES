As the event policy requires, we disclose that we used AI coding assistants during the hackathon:
- to scaffold and debug code (data loading, the training harness, the API and the UI);
- to help plan the work split between team members;
- in sections 3 and 4, to draft the explanatory text (model rationale, strengths/limitations, over-fitting discussion, selection and tuning interpretation) from our own results files; Member 2 reviewed it against `results/m2` and is responsible for it.

All experiments were actually run by the team. Every number, table and plot in this report was produced by the code in the repository; the corresponding CSVs, logs and plots are in `results/`. The analysis and conclusions are our own, and every team member can explain the code, the experiments and the results.
